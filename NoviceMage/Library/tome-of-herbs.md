# Tome of Herbs

## Moonpetal

Moonpetal grows only on the north wall of the tower and blooms for one night
every new moon. It must be picked with a silver knife, because iron makes the
petals turn black. Three moonpetals dissolved in warm milk cure the hiccups
caused by a failed levitation.

Never use more than three petals. Four petals make the drinker glow faintly
green for two days.

## Dragon's Thimble

Dragon's thimble is a small red flower that smells of smoke. It is the main
ingredient of the Salve of Calm Hands, which steadies a trembling caster before
a difficult spell. One thimble is enough for one caster. The salve wears off
after six hours, so a novice should apply it before the exam, not the day
before.
