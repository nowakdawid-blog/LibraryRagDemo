# Tome of Embers

## Fireball

A fireball needs a clear target and a steady hand. The caster must hold the
flame for exactly three heartbeats before throwing it. Novices who throw after
two heartbeats burn their own sleeves. Novices who wait for four heartbeats
burn their fingers.

The Council of Archmages limits the fireball to one cast per hour for any mage
below the rank of Adept. The flame leaves a smell of cinnamon that lasts until
the next morning.

## Frost Nova

A frost nova freezes everything within five paces of the caster, including the
caster's boots. The counter-spell is a warm bath, never a fireball. Mages who
answer a frost nova with a fireball have filled the tower cellar with steam
twice already.

The frost nova must not be cast indoors during winter. The Archmage's cat
refuses to leave the kitchen for a week after each such incident.
