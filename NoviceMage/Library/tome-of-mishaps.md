# Tome of Mishaps

## The Great Levitation of 1412

During the spring exam of 1412 a novice named Pip tried to levitate a barrel
of pickles. The barrel rose, the pickles did not, and the lid is still stuck to
the ceiling of the main hall. The Council decided that nothing heavier than a
sack of flour may be levitated by a novice without a master present.

## The Familiar Incident

A summoned familiar must be fed within one hour of its arrival. The raven
summoned by apprentice Lorn in 1388 was not fed, and it took the master's hat
as payment. Every familiar the tower has seen since then received a sardine
upon arrival, and the hat has never been returned.

## Rule of the Singed Eyebrows

Any mage who loses both eyebrows to a spell must report to the Archmage within
a day. Eyebrows regrow in about two weeks. A mage with missing eyebrows may not
sit on the Council during that time.
